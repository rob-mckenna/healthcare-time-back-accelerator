Ensure the synthetic nurse shift-closeout workflow is authentic, bounded, clearly non-production, and aligned to the StoryBrand outcome in `SQUAD_BOOTSTRAP.md`.

- Define the nurse journey, terminology, source-reference review, and approval/revision narrative.

- Metrics are `ILLUSTRATIVE`, not measured outcomes.
